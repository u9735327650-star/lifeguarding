What is Lifeguarding?

Lifeguarding combines proactive safety enforcement with rapid emergency response to prevent drownings and water-related injuries. Beyond basic supervision, modern lifeguarding relies on strict scanning protocols, specialised equipment and coordinated team responses.

What Do We Learn in Lifeguarding?

Lifeguard training teaches many important skills that help protect people in the water.

What Does Lifeguard Training Include?

In a lifeguarding course, you learn water rescue techniques, emergency medical care, surveillance strategies and accident prevention. Training combines classroom lessons with practical pool or open-water activities to prepare you for emergencies. You also learn decision-making, risk management and teamwork. These skills help lifeguards handle difficult situations and keep swimming areas safe.

Lifeguarding Equipment

Lifeguards use different equipment to help them supervise swimmers and respond to emergencies.

Why Do Lifeguards Need First Aid Skills?

Lifeguards need first aid and CPR training because they may be the first people to respond when an emergency happens at a swimming pool, water park or beach. They need to recognise emergencies, provide appropriate first aid and contact emergency services when necessary. These skills help protect swimmers and can save lives.
